# Website Copywriting Rules

## Purpose

This document defines the writing rules for all public copy on Farzam.nl.

The goal is to make every sentence feel human, strategic, premium, clear, natural, and trustworthy.

Farzam.nl must never sound like a generic marketing website, an AI tool website, a freelancer portfolio, or a social media manager profile.

The writing must support the core positioning:

Build a Growth System, not just more marketing.

---

## Core Writing Standard

All public website copy must feel like Farzam speaking clearly, professionally, and strategically to a serious business audience.

The writing should feel:

1. Human
2. Natural
3. Premium
4. Strategic
5. Calm
6. Clear
7. Executive
8. Practical
9. Trustworthy
10. Business-oriented

The writing should never feel:

1. Robotic
2. AI-generated
3. Generic
4. Overhyped
5. Artificially polished
6. Cheap
7. Pushy
8. Template-based
9. Too motivational
10. Too salesy

---

## Main Copywriting Rules

All public website copy must follow these rules:

1. Do not use dashes.
2. Do not use AI-sounding language.
3. Do not use robotic sentence structures.
4. Do not use generic AI phrases.
5. Do not use overhyped AI wording.
6. Do not use empty marketing slogans.
7. Do not write like a generic agency.
8. Do not write like a freelancer selling tasks.
9. Do not write like a social media manager.
10. Do not make AI the center of the brand.

---

## Important Clarification About Dashes

The no-dash rule applies to public website copy and user-facing writing.

This includes:

1. Headlines
2. Subheadlines
3. Paragraphs
4. CTAs
5. Buttons
6. Forms
7. Blog content
8. Course descriptions
9. Dashboard messages
10. Email copy
11. Newsletter copy
12. Microcopy
13. SEO descriptions
14. Error messages
15. Confirmation messages

Markdown documentation files may still use normal Markdown syntax when needed for structure, lists, file paths, and technical notes.

The rule is about published website text, not internal documentation formatting.

## Better Writing Direction

Instead of sounding like this:

Grow your business with powerful AI marketing solutions.

Write closer to this:

Build a clearer system for how your business attracts, converts, and keeps the right customers.

Instead of:

We create content that converts.

Write closer to:

We design the structure behind content so every message supports the customer journey.

Instead of:

Use AI to scale your marketing faster.

Write closer to:

Use AI where it improves speed, clarity, consistency, or decision making.

Instead of:

Book a call and grow your business today.

Write closer to:

Start with a strategic conversation.

## Headline Rules

Headlines should be:

1. Clear
2. Strategic
3. Direct
4. Human
5. Specific
6. Premium
7. Easy to understand

Headlines should not be:

1. Too clever
2. Too vague
3. Too long
4. Too hype-driven
5. Too AI-focused
6. Too generic

Good headline direction:

Build a Growth System, not just more marketing.

Where growth becomes clearer, execution becomes easier.

Turn scattered activity into a structured path for growth.

A clearer system for positioning, content, customer journey, and execution.

---

## Subheadline Rules

Subheadlines should explain the headline with more clarity.

They should answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What does the user get?
4. Why does it matter?

Subheadlines should stay calm and human.

Avoid long, overdesigned sentences.

## Page Copy Rules

Every page must answer three questions clearly:

1. What is this page about?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What should the visitor do next?

Every page must include:

1. One clear main idea
2. Clear section hierarchy
3. Simple explanation
4. Strategic value
5. A logical next step

No page should feel like random information.

No page should feel like a generic service page.

## Course Copy Rules

Course copy should make learning feel practical and strategic.

Course descriptions should explain:

1. What the course helps with
2. Who it is for
3. What the learner will understand
4. What problem it solves
5. How it connects to Growth Systems

Avoid language that makes courses feel cheap, exaggerated, or purely promotional.

Courses are part of the Growth System funnel, not random educational products.

---

## Form Copy Rules

Form copy must feel calm and professional.

Forms should not feel aggressive or invasive.

Use clear labels.

Use supportive helper text.

Good form direction:

Tell me where growth feels unclear.

What is the main challenge you want to solve?

What would a better growth system help you achieve?

Avoid:

Submit your details now.

Tell us everything about your business.

Get your free audit instantly.

---

Dashboard copy should feel like a premium user hub.

It should be:

1. Calm
2. Useful
3. Clear
4. Helpful
5. Structured

Welcome back.

Continue your learning path.

## SEO Description Rules

SEO descriptions must be clear and natural.

They should not sound like keyword stuffing.

Good SEO description direction:

Farzam Gholizadeh helps businesses build structured Growth Systems through positioning, customer journey design, content systems, AI leverage, and strategic execution.

Avoid:

Best growth marketing expert AI consultant Netherlands for business growth marketing automation and SEO services.

---

## Microcopy Rules

Microcopy includes:

1. Button text
2. Form hints
3. Error messages
4. Confirmation messages
5. Empty dashboard states
6. Login messages
7. Course progress messages

Microcopy should be:

1. Short
2. Clear
3. Human
4. Helpful
5. Calm

Examples:

Your message has been received.

Please enter a valid email address.

You have not joined any courses yet.

Start with a Growth Diagnosis.

---

## Copy Review Checklist

Before publishing any public copy, check:

1. Does it sound human?
2. Does it sound like Farzam?
3. Does it avoid dashes?
4. Does it avoid AI-sounding phrasing?
5. Does it avoid hype?
6. Does it support Growth System positioning?
7. Does it avoid freelancer or agency positioning?
8. Is the sentence useful?
9. Is the next step clear?
10. Would a serious business audience trust this?

If the answer is no, rewrite it.

---

## Final Writing Rule

The final standard is simple:

The text should sound like Farzam speaking clearly, professionally, and strategically to a serious business audience.

Every sentence should help the visitor understand the system, trust the thinking, or take the next step.
