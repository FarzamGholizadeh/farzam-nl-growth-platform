# About Page Blueprint

## Page

About `/about`

## Page Objective

The About page must build human trust and professional credibility.

This page should not feel like a traditional CV page. It should explain who Farzam is, how he thinks, why he builds Growth Systems, and why his background gives him a practical understanding of business, positioning, marketing, AI workflows, content systems, and strategic growth.

The page must support Farzam’s positioning as:

- Growth Strategist
- Growth System Designer
- Business-Oriented Thinker

Not:

- Traditional marketer
- Freelance marketer
- Social media manager
- AI tool operator

---

## Target User Intent

Visitors on this page want to know:

- Who is Farzam?
- Is he credible?
- What is his professional background?
- How does he think?
- Why should I trust him?
- Is he based in Europe / the Netherlands?
- Is he the right person for a serious collaboration?
