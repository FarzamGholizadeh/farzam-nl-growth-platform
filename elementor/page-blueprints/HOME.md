# Home Page Blueprint

## Page

Home `/`

## Page Objective

The Home page must introduce Farzam as a Growth Strategist and Growth System Designer.

It should quickly communicate that Farzam does not simply provide marketing or AI services. He helps businesses diagnose where growth is broken and design structured Growth Systems that align strategy, positioning, customer journey, execution, AI leverage, and scalable processes.

The page must route visitors toward the right next step:
- Learn the Growth System methodology
- Explore strategic modules
- View proof and selected work
- Read the blog
- Explore courses and webinars
- Complete Growth Diagnosis
- Book a Strategy Call

## Elementor Layout Notes

Use Elementor Pro with:

- Global colors
- Global typography
- Reusable section templates
- Clean containers
- Minimal nested structures
- Responsive spacing
- Mobile-first adjustments
- Light animations only
- Consistent CTA blocks

Recommended layout style:
- Full-width hero
- Controlled content width
- 2-column sections for explanation
- 3 or 4-column card grids on desktop
- Single-column stacking on mobile

---

## Motion Notes

Allowed:
- Calm fade-in
- Subtle slide-up
- Smooth hover states
- Gentle card elevation
- Subtle diagram line reveal

Avoid:
- Fast animations
- Heavy parallax
- Bouncing effects
- Flashy startup motion
- AI-looking effects
