## Page Objective

The Work page must build credibility through selected projects, experience, and proof points.

This page should not look like a traditional portfolio or CV page. It should show how Farzam diagnoses problems, designs systems, improves positioning, builds communication structures, and supports growth through strategic execution.

The goal is to prove that Farzam’s Growth System thinking is grounded in real experience.

Visitors on this page want to know:

- Has Farzam worked with real brands?

- What level of responsibility has he had?

## Section 3: Case Study Format

### Section Goal

Explain how each work example should be presented.

### Suggested H2

Each project is shown through the system behind the outcome.

### Case Study Structure

Every case study should follow this structure:

1. Context
   - What was the business or project situation?

2. Problem
   - What was unclear, broken, fragmented, or underperforming?

3. Diagnosis
   - What part of the growth system needed attention?

4. System Designed
   - What structure, positioning, workflow, content system, campaign, or UX direction was created?

5. Execution
   - What was implemented or guided?

6. Outcome
   - What changed or improved?

7. What This Proves
   - What capability does this case demonstrate?
