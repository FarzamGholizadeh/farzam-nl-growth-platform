# Blog Page Blueprint

## Page

Blog `/blog`

## Page Objective

The Blog page must act as Farzam.nl’s thought leadership and organic growth engine.

The Blog is not a place to write about SEO. It is a strategic content library where every article is SEO-structured, internally linked, and designed to attract the right audience, build authority, support social content repurposing, and convert readers into subscribers, registered users, course participants, or consulting leads.

---

## Target User Intent

Visitors on this page may want to:

- Learn how Growth Systems work
- Understand brand positioning
- Explore AI leverage without hype
- Improve customer journey and content systems
- Read Farzam’s strategic thinking
- Decide whether Farzam is credible
- Subscribe to the Growth Systems Letter
- Explore courses or book a strategy call
