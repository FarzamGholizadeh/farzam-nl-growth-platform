# Footer Component Blueprint

## Component

Global Footer

## Purpose

The footer must support navigation, trust, SEO, newsletter growth, and conversion without becoming visually crowded.

It should feel premium, calm, structured, and useful.

The footer should reinforce the core positioning:

Build a Growth System, not just more marketing.

---

## Footer Goals

The footer should:

- Reinforce Farzam’s positioning
- Provide clear navigation
- Support internal linking
- Capture newsletter subscribers
- Link to key pages
- Support SEO structure
- Provide legal and social links
- Maintain a premium visual experience

---

## Footer Structure

Recommended footer layout:

1. Brand block
2. Main navigation
3. Growth topics / Blog categories
4. Courses and resources
5. Contact / CTA
6. Newsletter signup
7. Legal and social links

---

## Design Notes

Footer should be:

- Light
- Spacious
- Minimal
- Grid-based
- Easy to scan
- Strong but calm
- Consistent with logo colors

Recommended layout:

Desktop:
- 4 or 5 columns

Tablet:
- 2 columns

Mobile:
- Single-column stack

---

## Motion Notes

Allowed:

- Very subtle section reveal
- Soft link hover
- Newsletter form hover/focus states

Avoid:

- Animated footer gimmicks
- Heavy effects
- Auto-moving elements

---

## Elementor Implementation Notes

Use Elementor Pro Theme Builder for the global footer.

Apply footer across entire site.

Use global typography and colors.

Keep footer reusable and easy to update.

---

## n8n Automation Entry Points

Footer should support:

1. Newsletter signup
2. Strategy call CTA click
3. Growth Diagnosis CTA click
4. Social click tracking if needed

Data to capture:

- Source area: Footer
- Source page
- CTA clicked
- Interest category
- Email consent
- UTM source if available

---

## Success Criteria

The footer is successful when:

- It reinforces the brand positioning
- It supports internal linking
- It helps SEO through topic links
- It captures newsletter leads
- It gives users a clear final conversion path
- It remains calm, premium, and uncluttered
