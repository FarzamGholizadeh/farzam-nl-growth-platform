# Farzam.nl Growth Platform Project Brief

Farzam.nl is a premium multi-page Growth System consultancy platform.

The website must not position Farzam as a traditional marketer, freelance marketer, social media manager, or AI tool operator.

Farzam is positioned as:
- Growth Strategist
- Growth System Designer
- Business-Oriented Thinker

Core message:
Build a Growth System, not just more marketing.

A Growth System combines:
- Business understanding
- Brand positioning
- Customer journey design
- Marketing execution
- AI integration when useful
- Automation and scalable processes

Marketing and AI are tools inside the system, not the core identity.

## Website Goals

The website must be:
- Multi-page, not one-page
- Premium, minimal, executive, and trustworthy
- WordPress + Elementor Pro ready
- Yoast SEO compatible
- n8n automation-ready
- Designed for lead generation, course registration, newsletter growth, and consulting conversion

## Blog

The Blog is not about SEO.

The Blog is a thought leadership and organic growth engine for:
- Growth Systems
- Brand Positioning
- AI Leverage
- Customer Journey
- Content Systems
- Business Clarity
- Personal Brand

All blog content must be SEO-structured, but the topic is not SEO itself.

## Courses

The Courses section should support:
- Free courses
- Paid courses
- Workshops
- Webinars
- YouTube audience entry
- Skool audience entry
- Lead magnets
- Email capture
- Course-to-consulting conversion path

## SEO

SEO must be applied structurally across the entire website:
- Heading hierarchy
- Keyword mapping
- Metadata direction
- Internal linking
- Schema-ready structure
- Fast-loading Elementor sections
- Goal: rank in top 3 results for targeted topics

## Motion

Use premium motion only:
- Calm page reveals
- Subtle hover states
- Smooth section transitions
- Minimal parallax
- Luxury micro-interactions

Avoid:
- Flashy animations
- Noisy motion
- Template-like effects
- AI-looking visuals

## Automation

The website must be designed with n8n automation entry points:
- Lead capture
- User registration
- Course registration
- Webinar registration
- Newsletter subscription
- Segmentation
- CRM pipeline
- Email marketing
- Content repurposing
- Sales conversion

## WordPress Context

The website will be built on:
- WordPress
- Elementor Pro
- Yoast SEO
- WooCommerce available
- n8n for automation

The implementation should be modular, reusable, responsive, SEO-friendly, and suitable for Elementor Pro.

## Final Direction

Farzam.nl should become a premium Growth Platform, not just a personal website.

It should combine:
- Personal brand authority
- Growth System positioning
- Consulting conversion
- Blog content engine
- Courses and webinars
- Registration and user dashboard
- n8n automation
- Newsletter and lead nurturing
